Complete Look — Mapoka Women

  1. Dress / skirt in Letsela (Mapoka) fabric
  2. White blouse
  3. Mogagolwane (blue tartan shoulder wrap)
  4. Tukwi (navy geometric head wrap)
  5. Black shoes
Note: The Letsela fabric may be made up as a full dress or worn as a skirt paired with a white blouse. Either style is acceptable.

The Celebration

Both Sides · All Guests

At the Wedding Celebration in Gaborone on the 11th of July 2026, all guests dress in the shared colour palette.

Women

Formal evening dresses or gowns in shades of burgundy, crimson rose, peach, blush, or champagne. The example shows a layered gown combining deep burgundy with blush tiers — this is the aesthetic to aim for.

Men

Formal suit or tuxedo. Burgundy or wine-toned jackets are on theme. Coordinate with champagne or blush waistcoats. Tie, bow tie, and pocket square should reflect the palette.

Colour Palette

Deep Burgundy Crimson Rose Peach Blush Champagne
