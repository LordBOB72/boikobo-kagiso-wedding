Dress Code

Dress with Love

Mapoka (Bride's Side)

Magadi & Kgoroso Events

  • Women: Letsela dress/skirt (blue & white geometric diamond print), white blouse, Mogagolwane (blue tartan shoulder wrap), Tukwi (navy geometric head wrap), black shoes.
  • Men: Navy blue two-piece or three-piece suit with a sky blue shirt. Tie & pocket square in Mapoka Letsela fabric.

Maun (Groom's Side)

Magadi & Kgoroso Events

  • Women: Letsela dress/skirt (dark ground with white dotted florals & gold accents), white blouse, Tjale (white fringed shawl), Tukwi (brown & orange head wrap), black shoes.
  • Men: Black two-piece or three-piece suit with a sky blue shirt. Tie & pocket square in Maun Letsela fabric.

All Guests

Wedding Celebration

  • Women: Formal evening dresses or gowns. We kindly ask that your outfit reflects our colour palette: deep burgundy, crimson rose, peach, blush, or champagne.
  • Men: Formal suit or tuxedo. We encourage you to incorporate elements of our colour palette (burgundy, crimson rose, peach, blush, or champagne) into your attire, such as through ties, pocket squares, or waistcoats.
